Notifications

Wording for order emails and the shop's WhatsApp alert — no code changes needed to reword these.

Shop Order Email

Sent to the shop's own inbox every time an order comes in.

Available: {orderId} {total}

Customer Confirmation Email

Sent to the customer right after they place an order.

Available: {orderId} {total}

Available: {shopWhatsapp} (becomes a clickable WhatsApp link)

Shop WhatsApp Message

Sent to the shop's WhatsApp for every new order (via CallMeBot).

Available: {orderId} {customerName} {customerPhone} {customerEmail} {address} {items} {subtotal} {discountLine} {shipping} {total} {paymentMethod} {paymentStatus} {shippingMethod} {deliveryLabel} {notesLine} — {discountLine}/{notesLine} are blank when there's no discount/notes.
